## Machine Learning pratctioners' pain points
ML Practioners terms could refer to:

| Key Concepts (Cues)| Detailed Notes| 
| ------------------| ---------------| 
| 🧑‍💻 Who is the "ML Practitioner"?|A broad term for anyone involved in the ML lifecycle (e.g., Product Managers, Data Scientists, ML Engineers).|
|⚠️ Managing Complexity| The challenge of tracking model and code versions, training procedures, hyperparameters, and performance metrics across different experiments.|
| 🤝 Multidisciplinary Collaboration| Operationalizing models requires alignment among Data Scientists, Data Engineers, ML Engineers, Developers, SREs, and business stakeholders.|
|🔄 Reproducibility| Critical for production deployment. Often required by policies/regulations. Allows teams to re-run the best model with broader parameter sweeps.|
|⚙️ Automation & Traceability|Automation reduces human error and boosts agility. Traceability is paramount, as production models require regular updates with new data.|
> 💡 Synthesis (Summary):
Transitioning an ML model to production goes beyond just writing code. It requires rigorous experiment management, cross-team collaboration, and above all, reproducibility and traceability ensured by automation, to guarantee the model is reliable, auditable, and adaptable to new data.
